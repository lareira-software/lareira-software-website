## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS in a single `index.html` (CSS and JS inline), self-hosted woff2 fonts in `fonts/`, images in `img/`. Hosted on GitHub Pages (repo `titina1503/lareirasoftware`) with `CNAME` → www.lareirasoftware.com. Must stay easy to maintain by hand by one person. (Pinned by the brief.)

## Users

Owners of small local businesses on the Costa da Morte (Cee, Corcubión, Fisterra, Muxía, Camariñas, Vimianzo), later A Coruña, the rest of Galicia and Tenerife: bars and restaurants, albergues and holiday rentals, hair salons, garages, shops, tattoo studios. They have no website or an outdated one. They look at it on their phone, often between customers. They are not technical and distrust agencies and monthly fees. Their job: decide in a few seconds whether these people are trustworthy and affordable, then send one WhatsApp.

## Product Purpose

Lareira Software & Design builds simple websites at a closed price for small businesses, plus Google Business Profile tidy-up and social media management. This site exists to get one action: asking for the free mockup (boceto gratis) on WhatsApp. Success = WhatsApp conversations started.

## Positioning

A team of two who live on the Costa da Morte and work with accommodations, surf schools and local businesses there. Before paying anything, the owner sees a free mockup of their own web, with their name, photos and Google reviews, in 2–3 days. Closed prices, domain always in the client's name, no lock-in. Framed as "we live here and want to boost local business", never against agencies.

## Operating Context

1. Owner writes on WhatsApp. 2. Lareira prepares a free mockup (no fixed turnaround promised; one mockup and one round of feedback per business) with the owner's name, photos and Google reviews. 3. Shown in a short videocall; if they like it, it continues; if not, nothing happens. 4. In 7–15 working days the web is live and on Google. Deadlines count from mockup approval, first payment and receiving photos, texts and access.

## Brand Commitments

- Name "Lareira Software & Design"; always signed "Lareira", never with personal names.
- Logo "lareira." lowercase with the final dot in orange must stay recognisable. Minimal hórreo symbol exists and may be used.
- Voice: close, direct, short sentences, "tú", no jargon, no grand promises. A Galician "Ola!" is welcome. Never against agencies.
- Must never look AI-generated: that is what they sell.

## Evidence on Hand

- No own photographs yet; leave slots for real Costa da Morte photos and client work.
- No clients, testimonials, figures or awards to show. Do not invent any.
- Example web shown must be a clearly marked invented business (e.g. "O Forno", bakery in Corcubión).

## Product Principles

1. Price and deadline visible before anything else: trust comes from numbers that don't move.
2. Show, don't promise: the free mockup is the product's proof.
3. Local and concrete: town names, real places, real habits, not slogans.
4. One action: WhatsApp. Everything else supports it.
5. The site is a sample of the work: if it looks templated, the pitch fails.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast on all text; secondary text with its own colour, never opacity. Mobile first, reduced motion respected, visible focus. Spanish primary (lang es); visitors may be older and read on small screens in sunlight.
